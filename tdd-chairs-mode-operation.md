# Chairs Modus Operandi

- Decide 2-3 of the chairs to to manage the process/speakers for a specific meeting
- Set a deadline for the speakers to create the slides so the chairs can review
- All chairs can review and comment on the slide
- We should be planning three meetings in advance
- Meeting once/month


# Guidelines for Presentations

- Assuming a sessions is 2 hours, allow 1.5 hours of presentation and 30 mins for Q&A
- Multiple speakers
  - Start with a broader view and/or presenting a use case (perhaps someone who is a consumer/user)
  - SMEs follow up on protocol related topics in the area
  - Presenters to coordinate on the topics to prevent duplication, be consistent and have a flow to the deep dive
- Best for speakers to be in person, perhaps based in the region where the meeting is taking place
