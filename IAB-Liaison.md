# IAB Liaison to the Technical Deep Dive (TDD)

The TDD is a cross-area venue for tutorials and technical presentations, with a focus on topics that span multiple areas. The IAB's architectural, cross-area vantage point makes it well placed to help identify such topics and to support broad relevance and balance in the program. A standing liaison provides a regular communication channel for that perspective, rather than relying on ad hoc interactions. 

The IAB established a standing IAB Liaison to TDD, appointed by the IAB, with a clearly defined role. 

## Expectations
The value of this role requires some engagement; a purely passive liaison is not deemed useful in this instance. The liaison is expected to:

- Provide the IAB perspective on topic selection, sequencing, and balance. 
- Serve as the standing communication channel between the TDD and the IAB. Report TDD activity and plans to the IAB, and provide the IAB's suggestions and feedback to the TDD. 
- Advise on candidate topics, with emphasis on cross-area subjects of architectural relevance.
- Offer assistance to the TDD Chairs in shepherding selected talks, identifying potential speakers, refining the scope, and reviewing material ahead of sessions. 
- Help capture lessons learned and identify opportunities to improve from past TDD sessions independently.
- Share relevant feedback from IAB outreach events that could feed into TDD session organization and content.

Note that the liaison is a role distinct from the TDD Chair(s). The liaison provides advice and facilitates communication between the IAB and TDD. The ultimate responsibility remains with the TDD Chairs and the responsible ADs.
